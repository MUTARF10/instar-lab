Our Mission

INSTAR Lab is an independent research organization dedicated to accelerating scientific discovery through interdisciplinary collaboration. We bring together researchers from diverse fields to tackle complex challenges at the intersection of science, technology, and society.

Founded in 2015, our network now includes over 300 scientists, engineers, and scholars working across institutional boundaries. We operate at the cutting edge of emerging disciplines, combining rigorous academic research with practical technological innovation to create meaningful impact.

Our unique model bridges the gap between academic research and real-world application, fostering collaborations that transcend traditional disciplinary silos. Through our global consortium, we're building the research infrastructure of tomorrow while addressing today's most pressing scientific questions.

Research Focus Areas

We pioneer interdisciplinary research across four key domains that represent the frontiers of 21st century science.

QUANTUM TECHNOLOGIES

Advancing quantum computing, communication, and sensing technologies through fundamental research and engineering innovation.

PLANETARY SYSTEMS SCIENCE

Integrating earth science, climate modeling, and space exploration to understand complex planetary systems.

COGNITIVE SYSTEMS

Developing next-generation AI that combines machine learning with insights from neuroscience and psychology.

OUR RESEARCH PHILOSOPHY

At INSTAR, we believe the most significant breakthroughs occur at the boundaries between disciplines. Our approach combines deep specialization with radical collaboration, bringing together experts who might never interact in traditional academic settings. We measure success not just by publications, but by real-world impact and the unexpected connections that emerge from our interdisciplinary work.
